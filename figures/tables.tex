\begin{table*}[t]
  \centering
  \footnotesize
  \begin{tabular}{clp{5.6cm}p{5.6cm}}
    \toprule
    No. & Flaw & Issue & Mitigation (controlled design) \\
    \midrule
    1 & Attribute priming &
      P103 is dominated by ``French'' (64\% of records); true contexts
      display the target's correct answer in 58--71\% of tuples, so a
      copy/induction mechanism can lower NLL without any truth encoding &
      anti-priming exclusion: no context sentence may display the
      target's correct attribute; dataset caps French at 40\% and
      matches attribute marginals via derangement \\
    2 & Answer leakage &
      context sentences are sampled independently of the target, so a
      context sentence can be the target fact's own true sentence &
      context sentences may not share the target's subject \\
    3 & Unpaired conditions &
      FF and TT tuples compare different random targets and context
      facts; fact-level difficulty variance inflates the boxes &
      paired design: the same context facts appear once as true and
      once as false sentences for the same target; paired
      $\Delta$NLL cancels fact difficulty \\
    4 & Tokenization &
      prompt ends with a trailing space while the target token also
      carries a leading space (double-space, off-distribution) &
      no trailing space; the target token owns the leading space \\
    5 & Stub built by \texttt{split()} &
      for multi-word attributes the stub retains words of the false
      attribute &
      stub rebuilt from the template and metadata \\
    6 & Unbalanced probe data &
      the layer-11 truth probe was trained on raw P103 (64\% French)
      and can partially cheat on attribute identity &
      probes trained on marginal-matched data \\
    7 & $p=0$ samples dropped &
      fp16 underflow makes some probabilities exactly 0; the resulting
      infinite NLLs are silently removed &
      logits saved; log-probabilities computed in fp32 via
      log-softmax; no samples dropped \\
    8 & Double forward pass &
      each measurement runs the model twice and discards one result
      (efficiency only) &
      single trace per prompt returns logits and activations together \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
  \caption{Flaws identified in the original Figure~6a pipeline and our
  mitigations. The faithful reproduction (Fig.~\ref{fig:fig6a})
  deliberately retains flaws 1--4 to replicate the original recipe; the
  controlled design behind Fig.~\ref{fig:behavioral-grid} fixes all
  eight. Flaws 1--5 affect the science; 6--8 are implementation issues.}
  \label{tab:flaws}
\end{table*}
